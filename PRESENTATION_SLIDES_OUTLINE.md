## 🎨 Design Tips

### Color Scheme:
- **Primary:** Blue/Purple (tech, trust, intelligence)
- **Accent:** Green (growth, success)
- **Text:** Dark gray on white (readability)

### Fonts:
- **Headings:** Bold, sans-serif (Montserrat, Poppins)
- **Body:** Clean, readable (Open Sans, Roboto)
- **Code:** Monospace (Fira Code, Consolas)

### Visuals:
- Use icons from Font Awesome, Material Icons
- Simple diagrams (avoid clutter)
- Consistent style throughout
- High-quality screenshots
- Data visualizations (charts, graphs)

### Layout:
- Clean, professional
- Plenty of white space
- Consistent alignment
- Bullet points over paragraphs
- Max 5-7 points per slide
